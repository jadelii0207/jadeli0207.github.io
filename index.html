Jade is a Chinese photographer and filmmaker based in New York, working across photography, film, and studio art. She approaches everyday life as a moving theater, finding dramatic tension in the mundane. As a director and cinematographer, she reverses this gaze using fiction to reveal the truths about the world it reflects. Moving between observation and invention, her work explores the elusive boundary between life and its representation.
